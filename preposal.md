# Preproposal

## What idea(s) do you have for your final projec?
1. Factory management
Build a factory management game with an optimization engine, random machine breakdowns, financial decisions, and live performance statistics.
2. Smart City Challenge
Players design traffic light systems, while an algorithm tries to improve traffic flow and reduce congestion. Include emergencies, rush hours, and changing traffic patterns.
3. Theme Park Challenge 
Manage a virtual amusement park, controlling ride capacity, staffing, admission prices, and customer queues.
Simulate hundreds of visitors with different preferences and use algorithms to improve wait times and satisfaction.
4. Robot Wars
Build a virtual warehouse where robots race to collect orders, navigate shelves, avoid collisions, and deliver packages.
Include different routing algorithms and let the professor design a warehouse layout to challenge the robots.
5. Smart Virtual Wardrobe 
Create a virtual wardrobe where users can upload and organize their clothes, generate outfit combinations, and receive personalized recommendations based on weather, occasion, color coordination, and style preferences. The program could also include a travel packing optimizer, outfit planner, and wardrobe analytics to help users make the most of their clothing.
